
# Airline Operations Knowledge Centre

## 1. Introduction

Airline operations involve coordinating aircraft, passengers, airport services, baggage, flight schedules and ground-handling activities to ensure safe and efficient air transportation.

## 2. Major Airline Operational Areas

### Flight Operations
- Flight scheduling
- Aircraft allocation
- Crew coordination
- Flight monitoring
- Departure and arrival management

### Airport Operations
- Passenger check-in
- Boarding gate management
- Baggage acceptance
- Airport coordination
- Ground handling

### Customer Operations
- Passenger assistance
- Booking support
- Schedule change notifications
- Flight disruption handling
- Special service requests

## 4. Airport Codes

Airports use three-letter IATA location identifiers.

Examples:

| Airport | Code |
|---|---|
| Delhi | DEL |
| Mumbai | BOM |
| Bengaluru | BLR |
| Chennai | MAA |
| Singapore Changi | SIN |
| Dubai International | DXB |
| London Heathrow | LHR |

Always verify airport codes before making a reservation.

## 5. Baggage Management

### Cabin Baggage
Items carried by passengers inside the aircraft cabin.

### Checked Baggage
Items accepted by the airline and transported in the aircraft hold.

### Special Baggage
Examples include:
- Sports equipment
- Musical instruments
- Medical equipment
- Oversized baggage

### Baggage Checks
- Weight allowance
- Piece allowance
- Dimensions
- Route restrictions
- Operating carrier rules
- Excess baggage charges

## 6. Flight Disruptions

Common disruptions:

- Flight delay
- Flight cancellation
- Schedule change
- Missed connection
- Aircraft substitution
- Diversion

### Disruption Handling Workflow

1. Identify the disruption.
2. Verify airline information.
3. Check passenger itinerary.
4. Review available alternatives.
5. Check applicable airline policy.
6. Communicate options clearly.
7. Complete authorised changes.
8. Document the case.

## 7. Schedule Change Management

Important checks:

- Original flight details
- Revised flight details
- Departure and arrival times
- Connection validity
- Passenger notification
- Rebooking options
- Airline waiver conditions

## 8. Special Passenger Services

Examples:

- Wheelchair assistance
- Unaccompanied minor services
- Special meals
- Medical assistance requests
- Assistance for passengers with reduced mobility

These services should be requested and confirmed according to airline procedures.

## 9. Airline Operations KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| On-Time Performance | Flights operating on schedule |
| Load Factor | Passenger occupancy relative to available capacity |
| Cancellation Rate | Cancelled flights relative to scheduled flights |
| Baggage Mishandling Rate | Mishandled baggage relative to baggage handled |
| Passenger Satisfaction | Customer experience measurement |

## 10. Quality Checklist

- Correct flight details
- Current flight status
- Correct passenger itinerary
- Baggage policy verified
- Special requests confirmed
- Connection times checked
- Disruption options communicated

## Disclaimer

Operational procedures and passenger rights vary by airline, airport and jurisdiction. Always consult current official policies and applicable regulations.
