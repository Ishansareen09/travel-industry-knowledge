
# Airport Processes - Complete Guide

## 1. Check-in

Check-in is the process of confirming passenger attendance and travel eligibility before departure.

Common activities:
- Passenger identity verification
- Booking verification
- Seat allocation
- Boarding pass issuance
- Baggage acceptance

## 2. Baggage Drop

Checked baggage is accepted, weighed and tagged for transportation.

Important checks:
- Baggage allowance
- Weight restrictions
- Destination tag
- Connecting flight requirements
- Restricted items

## 3. Security Screening

Passengers and cabin baggage undergo security screening according to airport and government requirements.

## 4. Boarding

Boarding involves verifying passenger eligibility and processing passengers onto the aircraft.

Important checks:
- Boarding pass
- Passenger identity
- Gate information
- Boarding sequence
- Final boarding deadline

## 5. Departure

After boarding and operational checks, the aircraft departs according to airport and airline procedures.

## 6. Arrival

Arrival activities include:
- Passenger disembarkation
- Immigration where applicable
- Baggage collection
- Customs where applicable
- Transit and onward connections

## 7. Important Operational Considerations

Airport procedures and cut-off times differ by airline, airport, route and aircraft operation.

Always follow current airline instructions.
