
# Training Analytics and Performance Dashboard

## 1. Introduction

Training analytics measures the effectiveness, participation and outcomes of organisational learning programmes.

## 2. Important KPIs

### Completion Rate

Completed Learners / Assigned Learners × 100

### On-Time Completion Rate

Learners completing by due date / Eligible Assigned Learners × 100

### Assessment Pass Rate

Learners Passing / Learners Assessed × 100

### Attendance Rate

Learners Attending / Learners Registered × 100

### Training Hours

Total learning hours completed by employees.

## 5. Dashboard Components

- Total learners assigned
- Total learners completed
- Pending assignments
- Overdue assignments
- Assessment performance
- Department-wise completion
- Monthly training trends

## 6. Reporting Best Practices

- Define KPI calculations consistently.
- Validate source data.
- Identify exclusions.
- Use consistent reporting periods.
- Protect employee information.
- Highlight risks and improvement areas.
- Share actionable insights with stakeholders.

## 7. Business Impact

Training analytics can help organisations:

- Improve training compliance.
- Identify knowledge gaps.
- Improve employee readiness.
- Measure learning engagement.
- Support operational performance.
- Make evidence-based learning decisions.

## Disclaimer

Training metrics should be interpreted with appropriate context. A completion rate alone does not prove learning effectiveness.
