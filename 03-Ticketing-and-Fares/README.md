
# Airline Ticketing and Fare Management

## 1. Introduction

Airline ticketing is the process of issuing electronic tickets for confirmed air travel bookings.

Fare management involves understanding airline pricing, fare conditions, booking classes, restrictions and applicable taxes.

## 2. Airline Ticketing Lifecycle

1. Flight search
2. Availability verification
3. Passenger booking
4. Fare quotation
5. Fare rule verification
6. Payment authorisation
7. Ticket issuance
8. E-ticket verification
9. Post-ticket servicing

## 3. Important Ticketing Terms

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| E-ticket | Electronic travel ticket |
| Ticket Number | Unique ticket document identifier |
| Fare Basis | Airline-defined fare identifier |
| Booking Class | Reservation inventory class |
| Validating Carrier | Carrier whose ticket stock is used |
| Coupon | Individual flight or service portion |
| Reissue | Exchange or replacement of an eligible ticket |
| Refund | Return of eligible ticket value |
| EMD | Electronic Miscellaneous Document |
| No-show | Passenger does not travel or comply with required procedures |

## 4. Fare Basis

Fare basis identifies a specific airline fare and its associated conditions.

Important factors:

- Booking class
- Passenger eligibility
- Advance purchase
- Minimum stay
- Maximum stay
- Seasonality
- Change restrictions
- Cancellation restrictions
- Baggage conditions

## 5. Ticket Reissue / Exchange

A ticket may require exchange when a passenger changes their travel arrangements.

### Reissue Checklist

- Original ticket validity
- Coupon status
- New itinerary availability
- Fare difference
- Change penalty
- Tax difference
- Additional collection
- Airline authorisation
- New ticket verification

## 6. Refund Processing

Refund eligibility depends on the ticket conditions and applicable airline rules.

### Refund Checklist

- Ticket number
- Ticket coupon status
- Fare refundability
- Cancellation timing
- No-show conditions
- Refund penalties
- Refundable taxes
- Ancillary service conditions
- Payment channel
- Airline approval where required

## 9. Common Ticketing Errors

- Incorrect passenger name
- Incorrect travel dates
- Incorrect booking class

## 10. Quality Assurance

Before completing ticketing, verify passenger details, itinerary, fare conditions, payment status, ticket issuance and customer confirmation.
