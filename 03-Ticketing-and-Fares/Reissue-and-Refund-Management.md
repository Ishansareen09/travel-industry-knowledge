
# Ticket Reissue, Exchange and Refund Management

## 1. Introduction

Ticket servicing involves managing changes, cancellations and refunds for previously issued airline tickets.

The applicable process depends on airline policies, fare conditions, ticket status and the reason for the change.

## 2. Types of Ticket Changes

### Voluntary Change

A change requested by the passenger.

Examples:
- Change of travel date
- Change of flight
- Change of destination, where permitted
- Change of booking class

### Involuntary Change

A change caused by the airline or an operational disruption.

Examples:
- Flight cancellation
- Schedule change
- Significant flight delay
- Aircraft change
- Airline-initiated rerouting

Involuntary changes may have different waiver and servicing conditions.

## 3. Reissue Calculation

A simplified illustrative calculation:

Additional Collection = Fare Difference + Applicable Penalty + Applicable Tax Difference

The actual amount depends on the fare rules and airline instructions.

### Example

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| New fare | ₹18,000 |
| Original fare | ₹15,000 |
| Fare difference | ₹3,000 |
| Change penalty | ₹2,000 |
| Tax difference | ₹500 |
| Additional collection | ₹5,500 |

This example excludes any other adjustments or charges.

## 4. Refund Management

Refund eligibility must be checked against the actual ticket and fare rules.

### Refund Types

- Voluntary refund
- Involuntary refund
- Partial refund
- Full refund
- Tax-only refund, where applicable

## 5. Refund Calculation Example

Illustrative example:

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Original amount paid | ₹20,000 |
| Applicable cancellation penalty | ₹3,000 |
| Other non-refundable amount | ₹1,000 |
| Estimated refund | ₹16,000 |

Actual refunds may differ based on ticket coupons, fare conditions, taxes, ancillary services and airline procedures.

## 6. No-Show Cases

A no-show occurs when a passenger does not travel or fails to comply with the airline's applicable check-in or boarding requirements.

Check:
- No-show penalty
- Ticket validity
- Coupon status
- Remaining flight segments
- Refund conditions
- Airline-specific restrictions

## 7. Important Operational Checks

Before reissue:
- Verify original ticket.
- Check ticket validity.
- Review fare rules.
- Check new availability.
- Calculate fare and tax differences.
- Obtain required authorisation.
- Verify the exchanged ticket.

Before refund:
- Verify ticket ownership and status.
- Check used and unused coupons.
- Review cancellation conditions.
- Calculate applicable penalties.
- Verify refundable taxes.
- Follow the correct refund channel.
- Document the outcome.

## 8. Voluntary vs Involuntary Comparison

| Factor | Voluntary | Involuntary |
|---|---|---|
| Reason | Passenger request | Airline disruption |
| Penalty | Fare-rule dependent | Waiver may apply |
| Fare difference | Usually assessed | Depends on waiver |
| Authorisation | As required | Airline rules may apply |
| Documentation | Change request | Disruption evidence |

## 9. Common Errors

- Incorrect penalty calculation
- Ignoring no-show conditions
- Incorrect coupon status
- Missing airline waiver
- Incorrect tax calculation
- Processing a refund on an ineligible ticket
- Not verifying the final transaction

## 10. Disclaimer

This guide is for educational purposes. Always follow the applicable airline fare rules, ticket conditions and authorised servicing procedures.
