
# NDC - New Distribution Capability

## 1. Introduction

NDC stands for New Distribution Capability.

It is an IATA-developed data exchange standard designed to support modern airline retailing and distribution.

## 2. Why NDC Was Introduced

Traditional airline distribution channels can limit how some airline products and services are presented.

NDC supports richer airline offers, including:
- Branded fares
- Additional baggage
- Seat selection
- Meals
- Optional services
- Personalised offers

## 3. Important NDC Concepts

### Offer Management
The airline or connected provider creates an offer based on the shopping request.

### Order Management
An order represents the purchased travel products and services.

### Ancillary Services
Optional services such as:
- Extra baggage
- Preferred seats
- Meals
- Priority services

### Servicing
Managing changes, cancellations and other post-booking activities.

## 4. Traditional Distribution vs NDC

| Area | Traditional Distribution | NDC |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution | Established industry channels | Modern airline retailing exchange |
| Offers | Existing filed fare content | Can support richer airline offers |
| Ancillaries | Channel-dependent | Can support richer merchandising |
| Orders | Booking and ticket records | Order-based concepts |
| Servicing | Depends on connected platform | Depends on implementation |

## 5. NDC Booking Workflow

1. Customer searches flights.
2. Shopping request is submitted.
3. Airline or provider returns offers.
4. Customer selects an offer.
5. Order creation is requested.
6. Payment and confirmation are processed.
7. Order details are delivered.
8. Post-booking servicing is handled.

## 6. NDC Challenges

- Different airline implementations
- Integration complexity
- Servicing limitations
- Payment and settlement differences
- Data standardisation
- Operational training requirements

## 7. Learning Objectives

- Understand NDC architecture.
- Learn offer and order concepts.
- Understand airline retailing.
- Compare NDC and traditional distribution.
- Understand the impact on OTA operations.
