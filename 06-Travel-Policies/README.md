
# Travel Policies Knowledge Centre

## 1. Introduction

Travel policies define the conditions under which airline tickets, baggage, cancellations, refunds and travel insurance are managed.

Policies vary according to airline, route, fare type, point of sale and applicable regulations.

## 2. Main Policy Categories

1. Cancellation policies
2. Refund policies
3. Flight change policies
4. Baggage policies
5. No-show policies
6. Travel insurance
7. Passenger rights
8. Special assistance policies

## 3. Airline Cancellation Policies

Important considerations:

- Refundable and non-refundable fares
- Cancellation penalties
- Cancellation deadlines
- No-show conditions
- Partially used tickets
- Airline-initiated cancellations
- Taxes and ancillary services

## 4. Baggage Policies

### Cabin Baggage
Items permitted inside the aircraft cabin.

### Checked Baggage
Baggage accepted for transportation in the aircraft hold.

### Special Baggage
Examples:
- Sports equipment
- Musical instruments
- Medical equipment
- Oversized baggage

## 5. Travel Insurance

Common insurance coverage areas:

- Medical emergencies
- Trip cancellation
- Trip interruption
- Baggage loss
- Baggage delay
- Flight delays
- Personal accidents

Coverage depends on the actual policy wording, exclusions and limits.

## 6. Passenger Rights

Passenger rights can cover:
- Flight cancellations
- Delays
- Denied boarding
- Refund eligibility
- Assistance during disruption
- Compensation where applicable

Applicable rights depend on jurisdiction and circumstances.

## 7. Policy Verification Checklist

- Identify operating airline.
- Confirm ticket issuing carrier.
- Review fare conditions.
- Check applicable market regulations.
- Verify travel date and route.
- Check ancillary service conditions.
- Document the official policy source.
- Record the date the policy was checked.

## Disclaimer

This is an educational reference. Always consult current official airline policies, insurance documents and applicable government regulations.
