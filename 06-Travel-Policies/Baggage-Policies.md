
# Airline Baggage Policies

## 1. Introduction

Airline baggage policies define the baggage passengers can carry during air travel.

## 2. Types of Baggage

### Cabin Baggage
Baggage carried inside the aircraft cabin.

### Checked Baggage
Baggage accepted at check-in and transported in the aircraft hold.

### Excess Baggage
Baggage exceeding the permitted allowance.

### Special Baggage
Examples include:
- Sports equipment
- Musical instruments
- Medical equipment
- Oversized items

## 3. Baggage Allowance Factors

- Airline
- Route
- Cabin class
- Fare family
- Frequent flyer status
- Ticket conditions
- Operating carrier
- Applicable codeshare agreement

## 4. Weight Concept vs Piece Concept

### Weight Concept
Allowance is determined by total permitted weight.

### Piece Concept
Allowance is determined by the permitted number of baggage pieces and applicable weight and size limits.

## 5. Excess Baggage Charges

Charges may depend on:
- Additional weight
- Additional pieces
- Route
- Cabin class
- Purchase channel
- Time of purchase

## 6. Baggage Handling Issues

- Delayed baggage
- Lost baggage
- Damaged baggage
- Missing baggage
- Incorrect baggage delivery

## 7. Baggage Verification Checklist

- Check airline baggage allowance.
- Verify cabin baggage dimensions.
- Verify checked baggage weight.
- Review special baggage conditions.
- Check connecting airline restrictions.
- Explain applicable charges.
- Confirm current official policy.

## Disclaimer

Baggage allowances and charges vary by airline and itinerary. Always verify the actual ticket and current airline policy.
