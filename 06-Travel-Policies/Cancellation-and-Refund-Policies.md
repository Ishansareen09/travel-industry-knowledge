
# Airline Cancellation and Refund Policies

## 1. Introduction

Airline cancellation and refund policies determine whether a passenger can cancel a ticket and receive a refund.

Eligibility depends on the fare conditions, ticket status, cancellation timing and applicable regulations.

## 2. Types of Cancellation

### Voluntary Cancellation

The passenger chooses to cancel the booking.

Examples:
- Change of travel plans
- Personal reasons
- Alternative travel arrangements

### Involuntary Cancellation

The airline cancels the flight or cannot operate the booked service.

Examples:
- Flight cancellation
- Significant operational disruption
- Airline schedule changes

## 3. Refund Categories

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Full Refund | Entire eligible amount returned |
| Partial Refund | Part of the eligible amount returned |
| Tax Refund | Refund of eligible refundable taxes |
| No Refund | No refund under applicable conditions |
| Involuntary Refund | Refund following qualifying airline disruption |

## 4. Refund Calculation Framework

A simplified illustration:

Eligible Refund = Amount Paid - Applicable Penalties - Non-refundable Amounts

Example:

| Component | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Amount paid | ₹15,000 |
| Cancellation penalty | ₹2,000 |
| Non-refundable amount | ₹500 |
| Illustrative refund | ₹12,500 |

This is an arithmetic example, not an actual airline refund determination.

## 5. Important Refund Checks

- Ticket number and status
- Used and unused coupons
- Fare conditions
- Cancellation date and time
- No-show provisions
- Tax refund eligibility
- Ancillary services
- Airline waiver conditions
- Original payment channel

## 6. Refund Processing Workflow

1. Receive cancellation request.
2. Verify booking and ticket.
3. Review applicable policy.
4. Calculate eligible refund.
5. Obtain required authorisation.
6. Submit refund request.
7. Track processing.
8. Communicate the outcome.

## 7. Airline vs OTA Responsibilities

The airline, ticket issuer and OTA may have different responsibilities.

Identify the seller of record, ticket issuer, payment channel and applicable servicing arrangement before advising the customer.

## 8. Quality Checklist

- Correct ticket verified
- Fare conditions reviewed
- Applicable penalty checked
- Tax treatment verified
- Refund amount validated
- Request submitted correctly
- Customer updated

## Disclaimer

Refund amounts and timelines are subject to the actual ticket, applicable policy, payment method and local regulations.
