
# OTA Operations and Business Models

## 1. Introduction

An Online Travel Agency (OTA) is a digital platform that allows customers to search, compare and book travel products.

OTAs connect travellers with airlines, hotels and other travel service providers.

## 2. Major OTA Platforms

| Company | Main Business |
|---|---|
| MakeMyTrip | Flights, hotels and holidays |
| Goibibo | Flights and hotels |
| Expedia | Global travel booking |
| Booking.com | Accommodation and travel |
| Trip.com | Flights, hotels and travel |
| Cleartrip | Flights and hotels |
| Agoda | Accommodation and flights |

## 3. OTA Business Models

### Commission Model
The OTA earns commission from suppliers for eligible bookings.

### Merchant Model
The OTA purchases or contracts inventory and sells it to customers under its commercial arrangement.

### Service Fee Model
The OTA may charge customers additional service or convenience fees.

### Advertising Model
Revenue may be generated through supplier advertising and sponsored placements.

## 4. OTA Booking Lifecycle

1. Customer searches for travel.
2. OTA connects to supplier or distribution partner.
3. Availability and fares are retrieved.
4. Options are displayed.
5. Customer selects an option.
6. Payment is collected or authorised.
7. Booking is created.
8. Supplier confirms the booking.
9. Ticket or confirmation is issued.
10. Customer receives booking details.

## 5. Post-Booking Operations

Common operational activities:

- Booking confirmation
- Name correction requests
- Date and flight changes
- Cancellation requests
- Refund management
- Schedule change handling
- Airline disruption support
- Payment-related queries

## 6. OTA Customer Support

### Important Responsibilities

- Understand customer concerns.
- Verify booking information securely.
- Check supplier and airline policies.
- Explain available options.
- Process eligible requests.
- Maintain accurate case notes.
- Follow escalation procedures.

## 7. Common OTA KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Conversion Rate | Completed bookings relative to eligible visits or searches |
| Average Order Value | Average booking value |
| Cancellation Rate | Cancelled bookings relative to bookings |
| Refund TAT | Time taken to complete eligible refunds |
| First Contact Resolution | Cases resolved in the first interaction |
| Customer Satisfaction | Customer feedback measurement |
| Average Handling Time | Average time spent handling cases |

## 8. Operational Challenges

- Airline schedule changes
- Fare differences
- Supplier response delays
- Refund delays
- Payment failures
- Duplicate bookings
- Customer expectations
- Policy complexity

## 9. Learning Objectives

- Understand OTA business models.
- Learn booking lifecycle management.
- Understand airline and supplier coordination.
- Develop post-booking servicing knowledge.
- Understand operational performance metrics.

## Disclaimer

Commercial models and operational procedures differ between travel companies.
