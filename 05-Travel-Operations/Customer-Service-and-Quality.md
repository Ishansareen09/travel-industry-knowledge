
# Travel Customer Service and Quality Management

## 1. Introduction

Customer service is a critical part of airline, OTA and travel agency operations.

It involves assisting travellers before, during and after their journeys.

## 2. Common Customer Queries

- Flight booking enquiries
- Ticket confirmation
- Name correction
- Date changes
- Flight cancellations
- Refund requests
- Baggage enquiries
- Schedule changes
- Missed connections
- Payment disputes

## 3. Customer Handling Framework

### Step 1: Listen
Understand the customer's concern without interruption.

### Step 2: Acknowledge
Recognise the inconvenience and show empathy.

### Step 3: Verify
Check the booking, ticket and applicable policy.

### Step 4: Explain
Communicate available options and restrictions clearly.

### Step 5: Resolve
Complete the authorised action or escalate.

### Step 6: Document
Record the case outcome accurately.

### Step 7: Follow Up
Provide updates when additional action is required.

## 4. Quality Assurance Parameters

| Parameter | Quality Expectation |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Correct information |
| Policy Compliance | Follow applicable rules |
| Communication | Clear and professional |
| Ownership | Take responsibility for resolution |
| Documentation | Accurate case notes |
| Resolution | Correct outcome |
| Data Privacy | Protect customer information |

## 5. Operational KPIs

### Average Handling Time
Total handling time / Total handled cases.

### First Contact Resolution
Cases resolved on first contact / Eligible cases × 100.

### Quality Score
Quality points achieved / Total applicable points × 100.

### SLA Achievement
Cases completed within SLA / Eligible cases × 100.

## 6. Escalation Management

Escalate cases involving:
- Complex ticketing errors
- Payment disputes
- Airline policy exceptions
- Fraud concerns
- Unresolved booking failures
- Safety-related concerns

## 7. Learning Objectives

- Develop travel customer service skills.
- Improve policy interpretation.
- Understand quality monitoring.
- Improve complaint resolution.
- Understand operational KPIs.

## Disclaimer

Use approved company procedures and protect all customer information.
