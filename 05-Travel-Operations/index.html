TOPIC 01

Online Travel Agency (OTA) Operations

An Online Travel Agency is a digital platform that enables customers to search, compare and book travel services. OTAs connect customers with airlines, hotels, car rental providers and other travel suppliers.

Major operational activities

  • Flight and hotel search and availability.
  • Fare and product comparison.
  • Booking creation and confirmation.
  • Ticket issuance and voucher management.
  • Changes, cancellations and refunds.
  • Customer support and supplier coordination.
Industry insight: OTA operations involve coordination between the customer, booking platform, airline or hotel supplier and payment processes.

TOPIC 02

Travel Booking Management

Booking management covers the complete lifecycle of a travel reservation, from the initial customer search through confirmation and post-booking service.

Typical booking lifecycle

  1. Customer searches for a travel product.
  2. Availability and pricing are checked.
  3. Customer details and booking information are captured.
  4. Payment is processed according to the applicable process.
  5. Booking is confirmed and ticket or voucher is issued.
  6. Post-booking requests are handled.

Common booking issues

  • Payment successful but booking not confirmed.
  • Duplicate booking or duplicate payment.
  • Incorrect passenger details.
  • Ticketing failure or supplier confirmation delay.
  • Schedule changes and involuntary disruptions.
TOPIC 03

Customer Service in Travel Operations

Travel customer service teams assist customers throughout their journey and help resolve booking-related issues while coordinating with airlines, hotels and other suppliers.

Important service skills

  • Active listening and clear communication.
  • Understanding travel policies and booking conditions.
  • Accurate case documentation.
  • Empathy and ownership of customer issues.
  • Timely follow-up and resolution.
Service principle: Keep customers informed, explain available options clearly and never promise a refund or change that has not been confirmed by the supplier.

TOPIC 04

Quality Management

Quality management ensures that travel operations teams follow defined processes, provide accurate information and maintain consistent service standards.

Quality monitoring activities

  • Call and interaction monitoring.
  • Email and chat quality audits.
  • Process and policy compliance checks.
  • Error identification and root cause analysis.
  • Feedback, coaching and improvement plans.
Continuous improvement: Quality findings should lead to coaching, process corrections and measurable improvements, rather than simply recording errors.

TOPIC 06

Escalation and Service Recovery

Escalation management is the process of transferring complex or unresolved customer cases to the appropriate specialist, supervisor, supplier or management team.

Recommended escalation process

  1. Understand the customer's concern and urgency.
  2. Review booking records and previous actions.
  3. Check the applicable supplier policy.
  4. Identify the correct escalation owner.
  5. Document the issue and actions taken.
  6. Communicate the next steps and follow up.
Management tip: Track the ageing of unresolved cases and establish clear ownership to prevent repeated customer contacts and missed deadlines.

Who should explore this section?

Travel consultants, OTA operations executives, airline support teams, customer service professionals, quality analysts, team leaders, operations managers and trainers.
