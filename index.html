About This Project

Travel Industry Knowledge Hub is an educational portfolio project created to demonstrate knowledge of airline operations, GDS platforms, ticketing, fare management, travel technology, customer service and learning operations.

All practical calculators and dashboards are educational demonstrations, not live airline systems.
