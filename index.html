ABOUT THE PROJECT

Knowledge Meets Practical Application.

Travel Industry Knowledge Hub is an educational portfolio project focused on the airline and travel technology ecosystem.

It brings together learning resources and practical demonstrations covering GDS platforms, airline ticketing, fare management, travel operations, technology and learning management systems.

The objective is to make industry concepts easier to understand through structured guides and interactive examples.
