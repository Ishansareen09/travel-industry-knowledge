Understanding GDS

What is a Global Distribution System?

A Global Distribution System (GDS) is a technology platform that connects travel service providers with travel agencies and other booking channels. It enables travel professionals to search, compare, reserve and manage travel products.

Key functions

  • Airline schedule and seat availability searches.
  • Fare shopping and itinerary pricing.
  • Passenger Name Record (PNR) creation and management.
  • Ticket issuance, changes and cancellations.
  • Hotel, car rental and other travel service reservations.
Industry insight: GDS platforms are important in traditional travel distribution, while modern airline retailing also uses direct connections and NDC-enabled channels.

Important GDS Concepts

CONCEPT 01

PNR

A passenger booking record containing itinerary and passenger-related information.

CONCEPT 02

Availability

Displays flight options and booking inventory available through a reservation system.

CONCEPT 03

Fare Pricing

Evaluates fares, taxes, rules and itinerary pricing before ticket issuance.

Who should use this learning section?

Travel consultants, airline operations professionals, ticketing specialists, travel trainers, quality analysts and people preparing for careers in travel technology.

Learning tip: Start with one GDS platform, understand the booking lifecycle, and then compare the workflows across other platforms.
